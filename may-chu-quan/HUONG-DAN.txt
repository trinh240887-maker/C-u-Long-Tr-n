MAY CHU O QUAN - HUONG DAN NGAN

1) Cai Node.js (nodejs.org, ban LTS).
2) Giai nen app-quan.zip vao C:\QuanLyQuan\app
3) Mo C:\QuanLyQuan\app\may-chu-quan, bam chuot PHAI "cai-dat.bat" -> Run as administrator.
   -> App tu chay ngam moi khi bat may, tu in bep/bar, tu sao luu moi gio.
4) Chuyen du lieu tu Railway (neu dang dung):
   App Railway -> Cai dat -> Sao luu -> "Tai toan bo du lieu ve may" -> chep file .db vao
   thu muc may-chu-quan -> chuot phai "khoi-phuc.bat" -> Run as administrator.
5) Router: dat IP co dinh cho may chu (DHCP reservation) de dien thoai luon vao 1 dia chi.
6) Google Drive tren may: them thu muc C:\QuanLyQuan\sao-luu-ngoai de co ban sao ngoai quan.

Cap nhat ban moi: giai nen de len C:\QuanLyQuan\app (du lieu nam rieng o C:\QuanLyQuan\du-lieu, khong mat),
roi khoi dong lai may.
Nhat ky loi: C:\QuanLyQuan\nhat-ky.txt
Ngung tu chay: go-cai-dat.bat
